Making rainy days fun and stylish

Welcome to Come Rain or Shine

Here at Come Rain or Shine, we turn rainy days into a fashion statement.

Why We Started

Come Rain or Shine was established in 2025. We noticed many people struggle to stay dry during rainy weather while still wanting to look stylish. We wanted to create a business that provides comfortable raincoats and umbrellas that protect people from the rain without compromising their confidence.

Our Mission

  • To make rainy days stylish and fun
  • To provide fashionable and quality umbrellas
  • To keep our customers happy and confident

Our Vision

  • To become a trusted umbrella brand
  • To bring comfort and style in all weather
  • To grow our brand across South Africa
